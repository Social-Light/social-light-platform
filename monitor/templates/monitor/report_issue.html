How these scores are calculated

Each mention is scored for sentiment on a −100 to +100 scale (0 = neutral) and weighted by the influence of the source, the prominence and relevance of {{ org.name }} in the item, and its audience. Issue-level risk scores aggregate these sentiment-weighted impacts across all mentions of an issue, where a more negative score signals a more severe effect on total sentiment. All scores are AI-assessed from the coverage and recalculated as new mentions enter the dataset.

How favourability is calculated

An AI-assessed index from 0–100 per stakeholder group, where 50 is neutral. Scores below 50 indicate coverage during the period is likely to erode that group's confidence; they combine the sentiment, volume and influence of the mentions most relevant to each group.

Our Methodology

1

Collection

Coverage is gathered across online, print, broadcast and social media sources.

2

Classification

Each mention is tagged by media type, source, country and reach.

3

Sentiment Analysis

Every mention — social, online, print and broadcast — is scored positive, neutral or negative based on tone toward the brand.

4

Valuation

Advertising Value Equivalency (AVE) and audience reach are calculated per mention.

5

Issue Filtering

The full period dataset was filtered to mentions relating to {{ report.title }}; all unrelated coverage was excluded.
