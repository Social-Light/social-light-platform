Getting started

From sign-up to your first brief.

Setting up takes about ten minutes and you can stop and come back to it — you return to the step you left. Nothing is charged during your {{ trial_days|default:14 }}-day trial, and we do not ask for a card to start one.

1

Start your free trial

Create your account with your work email, your name and your organisation. Your {{ trial_days|default:14 }} days begin immediately and no card details are asked for.

2

Confirm your email address

We send you a link. Confirming the address keeps your account, your alerts and your reports going to a mailbox you control.

3

Tell us who the account is for

Your role and country set the markets we monitor by default. Whether you are signing up for yourself or on behalf of an organisation or agency decides who is bound by the terms you accept next.

4

Read and accept the terms

Our Terms & Conditions, Privacy notice and Disclaimer are shown in full, one at a time, and accepted separately. Privacy is asked for on its own because it concerns your personal data rather than the service.

5

Choose your package

Pick the tier that matches your scope from our published price list — the same list for every client. Your package decides which features are available, and you can change it later.

6

Pay, and carry on where you left off

Pay by card through our payment provider, or ask us to invoice you for settlement by electronic transfer. Card details are entered on the provider's own secure page and never reach Social Light. Access continues the moment payment is confirmed.

Cancelling is just as straightforward — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Questions before you start? Email admin@sociallightbw.com.
